Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what personal data Lumina ("we", "the service") collects, why, and your rights over it. Lumina is a personal book-recommendation service that reads your Goodreads reading history to suggest books.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is [YOUR NAME / ORGANISATION], contactable at [CONTACT EMAIL]. For any question about this policy or your data, email that address.

What we collect

  • Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile-picture URL, and a Google account identifier — the openid email profile scopes only. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your reading data. The Goodreads CSV you upload — book titles, authors, your star ratings, dates read, and shelves. This reveals your reading habits and is treated as personal data.
  • Recommendation feedback. The 👍 / 👎 you give on suggested books.
  • A session cookie. A single signed cookie that keeps you logged in. It holds no tracking data and is not shared with third parties.
  • Basic technical logs. Standard server logs (e.g. request times) used to keep the service running.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your data.

Why we use it (legal basis)

We process your reading data and account basics solely to generate and show your personalised recommendations and profile — a service you actively asked us to perform. Under UK GDPR the basis is your consent (given when you sign in and upload your data), which you can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

Who we share it with

Your data is stored on our hosting provider, [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Fly.io / Render] (servers located in [REGION]), which processes it only to run the service. Sign-in is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. We do not share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and reading data until you delete your account or ask us to remove it, after which it is deleted within [e.g. 30 days]. Backups are rotated and purged within [e.g. 90 days].

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to: access a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to its use; or receive it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Data security

Access is protected by Google sign-in and served over HTTPS in production. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.
